🔗 Phrase Sharing Feature

⚠️ Important Sharing Information

When you share a phrase:

  • Only phrase text is shared - No app data, no personal info
  • Deep link included - Optional for recipients to add phrase
  • Standard device sharing - Uses Android's native share system
  • No tracking - We don't know who you share with or if they install
  • Recipient privacy - They only receive the text you choose to share
🔐 Your Responsibility: When sharing, the phrase text will be visible to recipients and subject to the privacy policy of the app/service you choose (WhatsApp, Messages, Gmail, etc.).

📁 Data Stored On Your Device

The app stores the following information exclusively in your device's local storage:

  • Custom Phrases: Text of phrases you create
  • App Preferences: Volume level, selected phrase, language settings
  • Default Phrases: Pre-loaded phrases in your device's language
  • App Configuration: Technical settings for proper functioning

💡 Important: All this data is stored locally using Android's internal storage system. It's never transmitted outside your device, not even to our servers (we don't have any!).

🎤 Text-to-Speech Processing

The app uses your device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine to announce phrases. Key points:

  • All speech synthesis occurs locally on your device
  • Only the selected phrase text is sent to the TTS engine
  • No audio recordings are created or stored
  • The TTS process is managed entirely by your device's operating system
  • We have no access to or control over the TTS engine

🔐 App Permissions Explained

These permissions are required for core functionality:

🔄 RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Purpose: Restart service after device reboot
Why needed: Ensures charging announcements work consistently without manual restart
Data access: None - only listens for system boot events

📱 FOREGROUND_SERVICE

Purpose: Run charging detection in background
Why needed: Detects charger connection even when app is closed
Data access: None - monitors system charging state only

🔔 POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

Purpose: Display persistent service notification
Why needed: Android requirement for foreground services
Note: This is a standard system notification indicating the app's background service is active.

🗑️ Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Uninstall the app: All app data is automatically deleted
  • Clear app data: Via device Settings → Apps → Hey, I'm Charging! → Storage → Clear Data
  • Individual phrases: Can be deleted within the app using the delete feature

We don't have access to your data, so we cannot delete it from our side (we don't store it in the first place).
