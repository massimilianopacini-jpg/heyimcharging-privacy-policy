Privacy Policy for "Hey, I'm Charging!"

📅 Last Updated: February 4, 2026

Effective immediately for all app versions.

1. Our Core Privacy Principle

Hey, I'm Charging! is built on a simple, foundational promise: your data belongs to you and stays on your device. The app is designed to function without collecting, storing, or transmitting your personal information to us or to any third parties.

2. Data Storage & Processing

All data is stored and processed locally:

  • Custom & Default Phrases: The text of all phrases is stored only in your device's local database.
  • App Preferences: Settings like volume level, selected phrase, and language are stored locally.
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): Speech synthesis is performed entirely by your device's system TTS engine. Phrase text is sent to this system component but is not stored or transmitted externally by our app.

Uninstalling the app will completely erase all this local data.

🔗 About the Phrase Sharing Feature

When you use the "Share" function, the phrase text is passed to another app on your device (like WhatsApp, Gmail, or Messages) using standard Android sharing. The privacy practices of that third-party app will then apply to the shared text. We do not control or receive any information about this sharing process.

3. App Permissions & Justification

The app requires the following permissions to provide its core functionality of announcing charging status, even in the background. No permission is used to access your personal files, contacts, or other private data.

🔄 RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Purpose: To automatically restart the background service after your device reboots.
Why it's needed: This ensures the charging detection works reliably without requiring you to manually open the app after every restart.

📱 FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE
Purpose: To run the charging detection service reliably in the background, even when the app is closed.
Why it's needed: A foreground service is required by Android to perform continuous monitoring (like listening for a charger connection) while the app is not in use. The "specialUse" type is declared to align with Android's requirements for such persistent utility services.

🔔 POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)
Purpose: To display a persistent notification while the background service is active.
Why it's needed: This is an Android system requirement for all foreground services, informing the user that the app is operating in the background.

🔋 WAKE_LOCK
Purpose: To briefly keep the device's CPU awake.
Why it's needed: This ensures the Text-to-Speech announcement plays completely and reliably when the charger is connected, even if the device screen is off.

🌐 INTERNET
Purpose: This permission is requested by the underlying Android framework or libraries used to build the app.
Important Clarification: Our app does not actively use the internet. It does not connect to any servers, send analytics, or fetch online content. The core functionality works entirely offline. This permission is not utilized by our app's own code for any network operation.

4. What We Do NOT Do

To be perfectly clear, this app does NOT:

  • Collect personal information (name, email, location, contacts, etc.).
  • Use analytics, tracking, or profiling tools.
  • Display advertisements.
  • Connect to the internet for its core features.
  • Access your files, photos, media, messages, or call logs.
  • Require an account or registration.
